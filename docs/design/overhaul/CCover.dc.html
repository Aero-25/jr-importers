Book a repair

A repair cannot be priced until a technician has the handset in front of them. So we do not quote over the phone and we do not guess. You bring it to the counter, we raise a job card, and the fault is written down in front of you along with what already works — screen, touch, ringer, charge port, cameras.

Anything over N$350 is confirmed with you before a technician starts. A minimum handling fee of N$200 applies and is non-refundable. Parts we replace carry a thirty-day warranty; keep the invoice, because no handset leaves the counter without its job card.

Most screens are back the same week. Water damage is honest work and honest odds — we will tell you which it is.
