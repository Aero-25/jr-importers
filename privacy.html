Privacy Policy

Last updated 13 June 2026

JR Importers ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect when you use our website, mobile apps and stores, how we use it, who we share it with, and your choices. By using our services you consent to the practices described here.

1. Information we collect

CategoryExamples
Account & contactName, email, phone number, password (stored encrypted), delivery address, town/region.
Order informationItems purchased, order totals, payment method and reference, delivery/collection details, order history.
PaymentCard payments are processed by our payment provider (DPO Pay). We do not store your full card number or CVV on our systems.
Device & usageDevice/browser type, app version, pages viewed, and a push-notification token if you enable notifications.
CommunicationsMessages, enquiries, repair/service details and support correspondence you send us.

2. How we use your information

  • To process, fulfil and deliver your orders and provide repairs and support.
  • To create and manage your account and verify your identity.
  • To send order updates, and — if you opt in — push notifications and promotional messages.
  • To detect, prevent and investigate fraud, abuse and security incidents.
  • To improve our products, services and store experience, and to comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information. We may disclose information where required by law or to protect our rights, safety and property.

4. Cookies & local storage

We use cookies and browser local storage to keep you signed in, remember your cart and preferences, and understand usage. You can clear or block these in your browser settings, but some features may stop working.

5. Push notifications

If you enable notifications (especially in our staff/admin app), a device token is registered with OneSignal/Firebase so we can send alerts. You can turn notifications off at any time in your device or browser settings.

6. Data retention

We keep personal information for as long as needed to provide our services, meet tax, accounting and legal obligations, resolve disputes and enforce our agreements. We may keep anonymised or aggregated data indefinitely.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures, including encryption in transit, access controls and authentication, to protect your information. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. International processing

Some of our service providers process data on servers outside Namibia. By using our services you consent to such processing, subject to appropriate safeguards.

9. Your choices & rights

  • You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, subject to records we must keep by law.
  • You may opt out of marketing messages at any time using the unsubscribe option or by contacting us.
  • To make a request, contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your identity first.

10. Children

Our services are not directed at children under 18. We do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a minor has provided us data, please contact us so we can remove it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version is always available on our website, and continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.
